Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account to play?
No. Tap "Play as Guest" on the home screen to jump straight into the core game. Creating an account is optional and only needed for the global leaderboard and saving progress across devices.
How does multiplayer work?
Memory Grid includes a real-time 1v1 mode where you can challenge a friend or get matched with another player online.
How do I change difficulty?
Choose Easy, Medium, Hard, or Pro from the home screen before starting a game. Difficulty controls grid size and how quickly tiles are revealed.
How do I delete my account or data?
Open Settings from the home screen and tap "Delete Account." This permanently removes your account and scores. You can also email ilyalcin@gmail.com and we'll delete it for you.
